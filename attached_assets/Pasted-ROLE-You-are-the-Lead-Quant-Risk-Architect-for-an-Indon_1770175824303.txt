ROLE:
You are the Lead Quant Risk Architect for an Indonesian stock intelligence platform.
Your responsibility is to protect retail users from speculative pump-and-dump stocks
("saham gorengan") by enforcing a hard safety engine.

TASK:
Implement an AUTOMATED GORENGAN DETECTOR that overrides Action Guidance and Readiness Score
when manipulation risk is detected.

This engine is mandatory and non-optional.

================================================
NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES
================================================

1. Gorengan detection MUST override optimism.
2. Gorengan detection MUST be deterministic (rule-based, not vibes).
3. When gorengan is detected:
   - Akumulasi Bertahap is DISABLED
   - Watchlist Prioritas is DISABLED
4. Action Guidance MUST be forced to conservative states.
5. All UI text MUST be in Bahasa Indonesia.
6. This engine applies globally:
   - Homepage
   - Stock Detail Page
   - Watchlist
   - Alerts (future-ready)

================================================
DEFINITION: SAHAM GORENGAN
================================================

A stock is classified as GORENGAN when speculative retail behavior
dominates price movement without institutional accumulation.

================================================
DETECTION LAYERS (LOCKED)
================================================

Implement FOUR independent detection layers.

------------------------------------------------
LAYER 1 — PRICE & VOLUME ANOMALY
------------------------------------------------
Trigger if ANY is true:
- Price increase > 25% within ≤ 5 trading days
- Repeated intraday spikes ≥ 10%
- Volume > 3× 20-day average

------------------------------------------------
LAYER 2 — BROKER FLOW FRAGMENTATION (CRITICAL)
------------------------------------------------
Trigger if:
- No dominant broker (Top 3 net buy < 35%)
- Many small brokers net buying
- Broker patterns are inconsistent day-to-day
- Retail proxy brokers dominate activity

------------------------------------------------
LAYER 3 — RETAIL DOMINANCE
------------------------------------------------
Trigger if:
- Small lot transactions dominate
- High frequency, low size trades
- Foreign flow is absent or contradictory
- No accumulation ladder detected

------------------------------------------------
LAYER 4 — STRUCTURAL FAILURE
------------------------------------------------
Trigger if:
- Market Regime ≠ Accumulation
- No tape control detected
- Absorption fails (price up but sell pressure strong)
- Distribution appears shortly after spike

================================================
FINAL GORENGAN DECISION RULE
================================================

IF (Triggered_Layers ≥ 2)
THEN
  GORENGAN_STATUS = TRUE
ELSE
  GORENGAN_STATUS = FALSE

================================================
OVERRIDE LOGIC (MANDATORY)
================================================

IF GORENGAN_STATUS = TRUE:

1. FORCE Action Guidance:
   - Allowed states:
     🔴 HINDARI DULU
     ⚫ KURANGI / EXIT (holders only)
   - DISALLOW:
     ❌ Akumulasi Bertahap
     ❌ Watchlist Prioritas

2. CLAMP Smart Money Readiness:
   - Maximum displayed Readiness = 59
   - Even if internal structural score is higher

3. ADD Internal Flag:
   - risk_override = "GOR"

================================================
UX REQUIREMENTS
================================================

- Display a clear warning label:
  "Aktivitas spekulatif ritel terdeteksi"

- Do NOT soften language.
- Do NOT hide this status behind clicks.
- Keep tone professional, not emotional.

================================================
VALIDATION CHECK
================================================

Before finishing:
- Verify gorengan stocks NEVER appear in:
  • Siap Dipantau
  • Watchlist Prioritas
- Verify homepage and stock detail ALWAYS match
- Verify no optimistic wording remains

================================================
FINAL OUTPUT
================================================

A hard safety engine that:
- Prevents false buy signals
- Protects retail users during hype
- Maintains long-term trust
- Works reliably under live market conditions

Proceed with implementation now.
