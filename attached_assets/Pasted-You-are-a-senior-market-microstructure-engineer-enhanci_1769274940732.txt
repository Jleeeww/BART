You are a senior market microstructure engineer enhancing an institutional-grade Indonesian equities intelligence platform.

GOAL:
Add a Smart Money Intent Engine that interprets WHY dominant brokers are acting, not just WHAT they are doing.

This layer must sit on top of:
- Broker Behavior Classification
- Tape-Control Detection
- Accumulation–Distribution Mode Engine
- Flow Quality Score
- Conviction Timeline

Do NOT add trading signals, buy/sell calls, or price targets.

--------------------------------
SMART MONEY INTENT ENGINE
--------------------------------

Create a new AI inference module that analyzes COMBINED behavior and outputs the most probable institutional intent.

Possible Intent Classifications (return ONE primary + optional secondary):

1. Inventory Building
   - Quiet absorption
   - Low volatility accumulation
   - Minimal price displacement

2. Inventory Exit
   - Selling into strength
   - Distribution masked by strong tape
   - Rising supply into rallies

3. Price Support Operation
   - Defensive buying near key levels
   - Tape-control characteristics
   - Broker recycling with low net progress

4. Mark-Up Preparation
   - Transition from stealth accumulation to active accumulation
   - Expanding net flow and improving Flow Quality

5. Liquidity Harvesting
   - False breakouts
   - Stop-run behavior
   - High churn with little net accumulation

--------------------------------
INFERENCE RULES
--------------------------------

Intent must be inferred using confluence of:

- Market Mode (from A/D Engine)
- Tape-Control Flag
- Broker Role Mix (Accumulator vs Distributor dominance)
- Flow Quality trend (improving, flat, deteriorating)
- Conviction Timeline phase

Intent should NOT change rapidly. Require multi-signal confirmation.

--------------------------------
OUTPUT FORMAT (BACKEND)
--------------------------------

Return:

smartMoneyIntent: {
  primaryIntent: string,
  secondaryIntent?: string,
  confidence: "Low" | "Medium" | "High",
  explanation: string (2–4 sentences, institutional tone, educational)
}

Explanation should describe behavior, not make accusations.
Use language like:
"Flow characteristics suggest dominant participants are..."
NOT "They are manipulating..."

--------------------------------
FRONTEND INTEGRATION
--------------------------------

1. OVERVIEW TAB
   Add a new card:
   🧠 Smart Money Intent
   - Show primary intent
   - Confidence badge
   - Short explanation

2. FLOW TAB
   Under Broker Summary:
   - Add line: "Dominant Institutional Objective: ______"

3. RISK TAB
   If intent = Inventory Exit or Liquidity Harvesting:
     Add caution note under Contrarian Risks

4. CONVICTION TIMELINE
   Use intent to influence phase explanation:
   - Mark-Up Preparation → strengthens Bullish Conviction
   - Inventory Exit → weakens late-stage conviction

--------------------------------
LANGUAGE RULES
--------------------------------

- No price predictions
- No trade instructions
- No hype language
- Must sound like institutional flow commentary

--------------------------------
RESULT
--------------------------------

The app should now explain:
WHO is active (broker roles)
HOW price is behaving (tape control)
WHAT phase the stock is in (market mode)
WHY smart money is acting this way (intent)

Proceed with implementation.
