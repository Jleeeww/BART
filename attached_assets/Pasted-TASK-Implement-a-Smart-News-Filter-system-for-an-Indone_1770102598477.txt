TASK:
Implement a Smart News Filter system for an Indonesian AI-powered stock intelligence platform.

This system must NOT be a news feed.
This system must classify, interpret, and FILTER news based on market impact.

The goal is to help investors focus only on news that matters
to performance, behavior, and risk — and ignore noise.

========================================
GLOBAL PRINCIPLES (MANDATORY)
========================================

- News NEVER generates buy/sell signals
- News NEVER directly changes Readiness Score
- News provides CONTEXT, not decisions
- All explanations must be Bahasa Indonesia
- Avoid hype, urgency, or sensational language
- Assume Indonesian retail audience

========================================
CORE DESIGN QUESTION
========================================

"Apakah berita ini mengubah FUNDAMENTAL, SENTIMEN, atau tidak signifikan?"

========================================
PART 1 — NEWS CLASSIFICATION ENGINE
========================================

Every news item MUST be classified into ONE category only:

----------------------------------------
CATEGORY A — FUNDAMENTAL-CHANGING NEWS
----------------------------------------

Definition:
News that can structurally affect valuation, earnings power,
or long-term business prospects.

Examples:
- Laporan keuangan (miss / beat signifikan)
- Right issue
- Akuisisi / divestasi besar
- Perubahan regulasi material
- Restrukturisasi utang
- Capex besar / perubahan strategi bisnis

Label (UI):
🔵 Berpengaruh ke Fundamental

AI Explanation Template:
"Berita ini berpotensi memengaruhi prospek jangka menengah hingga panjang
karena berdampak langsung pada struktur keuangan, strategi bisnis,
atau kemampuan menghasilkan laba."

----------------------------------------
CATEGORY B — SENTIMENT / FLOW NEWS
----------------------------------------

Definition:
News that affects short–medium term market behavior,
but does NOT change core business fundamentals.

Examples:
- Komentar manajemen
- Rekomendasi analis
- Rotasi sektor
- Sentimen makro
- Isu politik / kebijakan jangka pendek
- Rumor yang sudah tersebar luas

Label (UI):
🟡 Mempengaruhi Sentimen Pasar

AI Explanation Template:
"Berita ini lebih berpengaruh terhadap sentimen dan psikologi pasar.
Dampaknya cenderung bersifat sementara dan sangat bergantung pada respons pelaku besar."

----------------------------------------
CATEGORY C — NOISE (FILTERED)
----------------------------------------

Definition:
News that does NOT materially affect price behavior,
fundamentals, or institutional positioning.

Examples:
- Repeated old news
- Generic PR statements
- Optimistic wording tanpa data
- Clickbait headlines
- Social media speculation

Label (UI):
⚪ Informasi Tidak Signifikan

AI Explanation Template:
"Informasi ini tidak memiliki dampak struktural yang signifikan
terhadap fundamental maupun perilaku institusi."

DEFAULT BEHAVIOR:
- Category C news is HIDDEN by default
- User can toggle "Tampilkan Berita Tidak Signifikan"

========================================
PART 2 — FRONTEND STRUCTURE (CLEAN)
========================================

In TAB: BERITA

----------------------------------------
SECTION 1 — SMART NEWS SUMMARY (HERO)
----------------------------------------

Title:
"Ringkasan Berita yang Relevan"

Display:
- Number of Fundamental news
- Number of Sentiment news
- Noise hidden count

Example:
"1 berita fundamental • 2 berita sentimen • 5 berita disaring"

----------------------------------------
SECTION 2 — FILTERED NEWS LIST
----------------------------------------

Show ONLY:
- Category A
- Category B

Each news card must show:
- Headline
- Category label (🔵 / 🟡)
- Date
- Short AI interpretation (2–3 lines max)

NO long articles.
NO raw RSS dumps.

----------------------------------------
SECTION 3 — OPTIONAL NOISE TOGGLE
----------------------------------------

Collapsed by default:
"Tampilkan berita tidak signifikan"

========================================
PART 3 — CONTEXTUAL INTELLIGENCE (KEY)
========================================

Each news item must include ONE contextual tag:

Examples:
- "Berpotensi memengaruhi valuasi"
- "Dapat meningkatkan volatilitas jangka pendek"
- "Tidak berdampak pada struktur kampanye bandar"

This tag connects news to:
- Market Regime
- Flow behavior
- Risk context

----------------------------------------
IMPORTANT:
----------------------------------------

News must NEVER override:
- Market Regime
- Flow analysis
- Readiness Score

If news contradicts flow:
AI must say:
"Respons pasar terhadap berita ini masih terbatas dan belum tercermin
dalam perilaku bandar."

========================================
PART 4 — AI LANGUAGE RULES
========================================

ALLOWED:
- "berpotensi"
- "dapat memengaruhi"
- "tergantung respons pasar"
- "belum mengubah struktur"

FORBIDDEN:
- "positif untuk saham"
- "katalis kuat"
- "sinyal beli"
- "akan mendorong harga"

========================================
PART 5 — FUTURE-PROOFING (DO NOT IMPLEMENT NOW)
========================================

Prepare architecture hooks for:
- News vs Bandar Reaction analysis
- News absorption tracking
- News impact decay over time

DO NOT activate these yet.

========================================
FINAL CHECK
========================================

Before completion, ensure:
- News tab feels calm, not urgent
- Noise is reduced, not amplified
- Retail users are guided, not provoked
- App remains behavior-first, not headline-driven

If any news logic currently shows raw headlines without interpretation,
REPLACE it with this Smart News Filter system.

OUTPUT:
A disciplined, intelligent Smart News Filter that protects users
from noise while highlighting what actually matters.
